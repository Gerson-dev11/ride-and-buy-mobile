## Overview

Ride & Buy Mobile is a mobile car rental platform developed with Flutter and Dart. The application connects users with nearby rental businesses based on their current location, allowing them to discover available vehicles within a 30 km radius.

The platform combines location-based search, multi-step identity verification, facial recognition, AI-assisted vehicle validation, digital payments, QR-based rental verification, and role-based functionality for customers and rental business administrators.
